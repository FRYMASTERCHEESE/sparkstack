What Bitcoin is (plain English)

Bitcoin is digital value that no single bank prints. You hold it with a wallet you control. If you lose the seed phrase, nobody can reset it. If you send coins to the wrong address, they do not come back. That is why SparkStack never asks for a seed and never holds coins for you.

How “free crypto” apps really work

Advertisers pay networks such as Google. The network pays the site owner a cut. The owner may share some of that as points or later as tiny withdrawals. If there are no visitors and no approved ads, there is no budget. Anyone promising unlimited free Bitcoin with no ads is not telling the truth.
