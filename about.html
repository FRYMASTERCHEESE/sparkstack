About

SparkStack is a free English website for people who are new to crypto.

It teaches the difference between a public address and a seed phrase, why “free crypto” apps need advertisers, and how to watch a wallet you already own.

Practice points are stored only on your device. They are not a currency and not an investment.

The operator does not custody digital assets and does not give personalised financial advice.

Live site: https://frymastercheese.github.io/sparkstack/
