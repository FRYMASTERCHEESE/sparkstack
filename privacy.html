Privacy

SparkStack is a beginner demo. English only.

  • Points, notes, and a watch address stay in your browser (local storage). We do not have a server that stores them.
  • Market prices are fetched from CoinGecko. That service may see that someone requested prices.
  • Do not enter seed phrases, passwords, or private keys.
  • When Google AdSense or an offer wall is connected, those companies collect ad and device data under their own policies. We will name them here after they are live.
  • This app does not custody cryptocurrency and does not send coins to the developer.

This is a simple notice for a demo site, not legal advice.
